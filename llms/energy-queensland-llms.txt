# Energy Queensland

> Energy Queensland Limited is the Queensland Government-owned corporation that owns Energex and Ergon Energy Network (2.3 million electricity connections, distribution), Ergon Energy Retail (the notified-price retailer for ~760,000 regional Queensland customers), Yurika and SPARQ Solutions. It has no developer portal and publishes no API of its own. Every machine-readable surface recorded here exists because Part IVD of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 designated the RETAIL subsidiary as a Consumer Data Right energy data holder. The distribution half was never designated and publishes nothing programmable.

Generated by API Evangelist (apievangelist.com) from https://github.com/api-evangelist/energy-queensland — method: generated, 2026-07-27. No provider-published llms.txt exists: /llms.txt was not reachable on any Energy Queensland host (the corporate sites return HTTP 403 to every programmatic client behind a Cloudflare challenge).

## What is actually callable

- [Get Generic Plans](https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/ergon/cds-au/v1/energy/plans): Anonymous. Send header `x-v: 1`. Returns Ergon Energy Retail's 36 REGULATED electricity plans for regional Queensland. Hosted by the Australian Energy Regulator's Energy Made Easy CDR host, not by Energy Queensland — in CDR energy (unlike CDR banking) generic plan data is served centrally per brand.
- [Get Generic Plan Detail](https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/ergon/cds-au/v1/energy/plans/ERG1064038RRE1@EME): Anonymous. Requires header `x-v: 3` (x-v 1 returns HTTP 406). Full tariff, supply charge, solar feed-in, discount and eligibility detail.
- [Get Status](https://public.cdr.ergonretail.com.au/cds-au/v1/discovery/status): Anonymous. `x-v: 1`. The only anonymous endpoint on an Energy-Queensland-controlled host. Returns OK / PARTIAL_FAILURE / SCHEDULED_OUTAGE / UNAVAILABLE.
- [Get Outages](https://public.cdr.ergonretail.com.au/cds-au/v1/discovery/outages): Anonymous. `x-v: 1`. Advertised CDR outages.

Everything else — energy accounts, balances, billing, invoices, concessions, payment schedules, electricity service points, interval usage and distributed energy resources (23 operations) — requires ACCC accreditation as a CDR data recipient, mutual TLS with CDR Register certificates, FAPI OAuth2/OIDC, and the individual customer's consent. There is no sandbox, no API key, no partner tier and no commercial path.

## APIs

- [Ergon Energy Retail CDR Energy Product Reference Data API](https://consumerdatastandardsaustralia.github.io/standards/#energy-apis): Anonymous generic plan data for the "ergon" brand.
- [Ergon Energy Retail CDR Discovery API](https://consumerdatastandardsaustralia.github.io/standards/#common-apis): Get Status and Get Outages on Ergon's registered CDR public base URI.
- [Ergon Energy Retail CDR Energy Consumer Data API](https://consumerdatastandardsaustralia.github.io/standards/#energy-apis): The consumer-authorised surface. Accredited data recipients only; the infosec base URI is published through the authenticated CDR Register.

## What does not exist

- No developer portal, on any group domain. developer., developers., api., docs., data. and opendata. are NXDOMAIN on energyq.com.au, ergon.com.au and energex.com.au.
- No SDKs or client libraries on npm, PyPI, Maven Central, NuGet, pkg.go.dev, RubyGems, Packagist or crates.io.
- No CLI, no UI components, no Postman collection, no sandbox, no MCP server.
- No security.txt, no /.well-known document of any kind (all 404 on the CDR hosts, all 403 on the corporate sites).
- No AsyncAPI, no webhooks, no event or streaming surface.
- No open grid, network or market data API. Energex and Ergon Energy Network — 2.3 million connections — publish PDF planning reports, XLSX annexures, map viewers, and one open-licensed GIS dataset deposited on the Queensland Government's CKAN portal at data.qld.gov.au (which is a third party's API, not theirs).
- No published security certification (no SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS) and no trust centre.
